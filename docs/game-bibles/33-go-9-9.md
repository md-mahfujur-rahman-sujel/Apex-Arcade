# Game 33 — Go 9×9

## Pitch

A complete Go table on a warm kaya board. Classic is 9×9 with 7.5 komi; Quick drops to a dense 7×7, Grand opens a 13×13 board, Blitz adds a 90-second clock and Master runs the deepest search. The game carries real liberties, capture, ko protection, pass-and-score territory counting and a click-to-mark dead-stone review, so it plays like Go rather than a simplified capture game.

## Art direction

A warm wood board with thin dark grid lines, star points, and black and white stones shaded from an upper-left highlight. The board sits on a `#5a3b16` frame with a deep contact shadow. The last stone gets a contrast ring; a hint pulses in the accent colour. During scoring, dead stones are dimmed with a red cross and surrounded territory is marked with small black or white squares. The menu scene uses the shared stones motif: scattered stone circles under a neutral glow. Lite and reduced motion keep stones, lines, last-move ring and territory marks while dropping the placement pop.

## Modes and match structure

| Mode | Board | Komi | Clock | Score multiplier | Win bonus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Classic | 9×9 | 7.5 | none | 1× | 1,500 |
| Blitz | 9×9 | 7.5 | 1:30 per side | 1.1× | 1,200 |
| Quick | 7×7 | 6.5 | none | 1.2× | 1,000 |
| Grand | 13×13 | 7.5 | none | 1.3× | 1,800 |
| Master | 9×9 | 7.5 | none | 1.5× | 2,000 |

1. **Classic:** the standard 9×9 game; area scoring after two consecutive passes.
2. **Blitz:** ninety seconds per side. The clock pauses with the menu; a flag loses with no bonus.
3. **Quick:** a 7×7 board with 6.5 komi, dense and tactical.
4. **Grand:** a 13×13 board with more room for influence and large fights.
5. **Master:** locked until one Classic win (`classicWins ≥ 1`); the computer always runs the deepest search.

A mode change starts a fresh board. Pause/resume preserves stones, captures, passes, clock and undo history.

## Core rules

- Black moves first. Stones are placed on empty intersections.
- A connected group with no empty neighbouring point (liberties) is captured and removed.
- A move that would leave its own group with no liberties is illegal unless it captures first.
- Repeating an earlier board position is illegal (ko).
- Passing is always legal. Two consecutive passes open the scoring phase.
- In scoring, dead stones are marked from a simulated-play guess and any group can be toggled by clicking it.
- Area score is stones on the board plus empty points surrounded only by that colour. White adds komi.
- Undo rewinds a move, pass, capture, score change and the position history together.

## Scoring

| Event | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Place a stone | 2 |
| Capture a stone | 12 per stone |
| Win | Mode bonus |

The final score is `(points earned + win bonus) × mode multiplier`, rounded. A timeout win awards no bonus. The mode bonus is paid only to the human seat on a win; a loss records no bonus. Area and komi decide the winner, not the point total.

## Records and persistence

Saved under `go` through `Save` / `GameUI.record`:

- `bestClassicScore`, `bestBlitzScore`, `bestQuickScore`, `bestGrandScore`, `bestMasterScore`
- `bestScore`, `bestArea`, `bestCapture`
- `wins`, `classicWins`, `blitzWins`, `quickWins`, `grandWins`, `masterWins`
- `stonesPlaced`, `stonesCaptured`
- `fewestMoves` on a win
- `plays`, `secondsPlayed`, `lastResult`, `lastMode`

`bestArea` stores the human side's largest final area; `bestCapture` stores the largest single capture.

## Menu and controls

The page mounts the shared board menu (Play, Modes, Customize, Options, About, Stats, Exit).

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Click / tap an intersection | Place a stone |
| P | Pass |
| T | Show a suggested move |
| U | Undo |
| N | New game |
| H / M | Shared help / mute |

The side rail carries status, the two area cards, seat and level selectors, and New game / Pass / Undo / Hint. The Confirm score button appears only in the scoring phase. Clicking a group during scoring toggles its dead status.
