# Game 42 — Merge 2048

## Pitch

At the Elemental Foundry, combine matching cores to awaken the Prime Core. Each run is a compact puzzle expedition: build toward 2048, decide whether to bank the victory or continue, and improve your best score.

## Art direction

Keep the familiar sliding-tile puzzle language, refined to fit Apex Arcade: dark slate field, warm ivory low-value tiles, then a clear coral-to-violet/cyan-to-amber energy progression for larger values. Use a soft upper-left light, crisp tile edges, subtle contact shadows, and restrained merge glow. Preserve high-contrast numbers and readable 3×3 through 6×6 boards. Short slides and merge pops; respect reduced motion.

## Characters and story beats

The Foundry Keeper introduces the rules, then names each milestone as a Core tier. A run begins with two cores, builds through merges, reaches the Prime Core at 2048, and offers a keep-going choice. A no-moves result returns to the Foundry with score, best tile, and a run record. Daily trials provide a seeded alternate route.

## Modes and progression

- Classic Run: 4×4 board; reach 2048, then continue or finish.
- Compact: 3×3 board for short, high-pressure runs.
- Spacious: 5×5 and 6×6 boards for longer planning and larger chains.
- Foundry Trials: 30 escalating tile targets with move limits; clear a trial to earn stars and save progress.
- Daily Core: deterministic date seed and goal, with a local best score for each date.

## Difficulty contract

| Setting | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | 82% chance of a 2 spawn and up to 8 undos. |
| Normal | Standard 90% chance of a 2 spawn and up to 4 undos. |
| Hard | 97% chance of a 2 spawn and one undo. |

Difficulty changes spawn odds and undo allowance only; it does not silently change board size. The setting and remaining undos stay visible during play.

## Controls

Arrow keys or WASD; swipe on touch. U undoes an allowed move; N starts a new run. M mutes audio and H opens controls. The grid remains swipe-friendly without changing the familiar puzzle interaction.

## Achievements

First Merge; Eightfold (tile 8); Century Core (tile 128); Prime Core (2048); Beyond the Core (continue past 2048); Chain Reaction (merge twice in one move); Clean Foundry (win without undo); Daily Spark (complete a daily run).

## Features and risks

Preserve the familiar merge rules and standalone launch. Each settled run autosaves locally and offers resume on return; Save stores per-size best scores, aggregate run stats, completed trials, trial stars, and unlocked achievements. The statistics view shows these records. Report session start/end, score milestones, trial completion, and achievements through score-bridge when available. Risks: seeded retry behavior, challenge balance, and touch controls still need browser testing. Keep the merge rules unchanged during visual polish.
